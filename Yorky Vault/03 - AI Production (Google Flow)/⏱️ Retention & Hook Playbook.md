# ⏱️ Retention & Hook Playbook: Gen Z Optimization

> [!WARNING] The 1.5-Second Scroll Reality
> Gen Z viewers swipe away within 1.5 to 2.5 seconds if an immediate tension anchor is missing. Sluggish 7-second ramp-ups result in dead videos.

---

## ⚡ 1. The 3-Second Hook Rule

* The core pain point, question, or contradiction **MUST land between 0:00 and 0:03** (words 1 to 6).
* ❌ *Slow (Rejected)*: *"October aa chuka hai aur dimag mein bas ek hi tension..."* (Boring ramp-up)
* ✅ *Fast (Approved)*: *"October me 40% syllabus bacha hai? Suno!"* / *"100% syllabus ke chakkar me J.E.E barbaad mat karna!"*

---

## 🗣️ 2. The Phonetics Rule: `J.E.E.` vs. `Ji`

* **The Problem**: AI voice synthesizers read "JEE" as the Hindi honorific **"जी"** (*"jee haan"*).
* **The Fix**: In every voiceover script and prompt, **ALWAYS write `J-E-E` or `J.E.E.`** (never plain `JEE`).

---

## 🚀 3. High-BPM Cadence & Zero Dead Air

* Gen Z watches content at 1.5x by default. Normal speech feels sluggish.
* Target **80–85 tight words** per 30-second Short (~2.8 words/sec).
* Eliminate lingering 0.8s silences between sentences.
