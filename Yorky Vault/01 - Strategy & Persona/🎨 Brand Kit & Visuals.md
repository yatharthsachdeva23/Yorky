# 🎨 Brand Kit & Visual Identity: "The Signal in the Noise"

> [!TIP] Aesthetic Principle
> While every other JEE channel screams with clickbait red arrows and shock faces, Yatharth's visual grammar is **engineering-grade, calm, precise, and premium**—like Linear, Vercel, or Stripe applied to JEE education.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette

| Color Name | Hex Code | Ratio | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Deep Obsidian** | `#0A0B10` | 60% | Primary dark background (YouTube dark mode native) |
| **Electric Teal** | `#00D4AA` | 30% | Signature brand accent, highlight boxes, key text |
| **Neon Mint** | `#00FF88` | 10% | Secondary pop, success markers, metrics |
| **Pure White** | `#FFFFFF` | Text | Primary typography and headings |
| **Cool Grey** | `#8B93A3` | Borders | Muted secondary text, outlines, grid dividers |

---

## 📏 The "Signal Bar" (Core Visual Grammar)

* **Design**: A clean **4px horizontal line in Electric Teal (`#00D4AA`)** positioned at the top or above key titles.
* **Function**: Provides **1-second instant recognition** across all thumbnails, Shorts, and Community posts without needing an obtrusive logo.

---

## 🔤 Typography

* **Headlines**: `Space Grotesk` (Bold / ExtraBold) — gives an engineering, geometric, high-precision edge.
* **Body / Subtitles**: `Inter` / `Montserrat` — maximum mobile legibility under rapid scroll.
