# 🎯 Audience Playbook: Class 12 JEE Aspirants

> [!NOTE] Target Cohort
> Students in **Class 12th preparing for JEE Main / Advanced 2027-2028**. They are under simultaneous pressure from schools (Boards/attendance) and coaching centers.

---

## 🧠 The October Psychological Crisis

In early October, every 12th-grade aspirant experiences severe panic:
1. **Backlog Overload**: 11th mechanics and chemistry backlogs are mounting, while 12th syllabus is moving at lightning speed.
2. **Syllabus Deadline Fear**: Coaching announces "Syllabus ends in December", but students realize they haven't revised early topics.
3. **The 75% Board Attendance Dilemma**: Schools start demanding practical files, pre-board attendance, and lab work.

---

## 💡 Content Strategy Pillars

* **70% Problem-Solving & PYQ Drills**: High shareability, counter-intuitive tricks, quick formula applications.
* **20% High-Density Strategy & Roadmaps**: Breaking down syllabus traps, realistic time splits, mock test analysis.
* **10% Tough-Love Mental Framing**: Brotherly reality checks, consistency motivation without toxic positivity.
