## 🛡️ Production Quality Gates (Checklist)

- [ ] **3-Second Hook Rule**: Does the core panic/punch land by second 3?
- [ ] **J.E.E. Phonetics**: Is `J.E.E.` spelled with hyphens/periods to prevent "ji" bug?
- [ ] **Camera Continuity**: Medium (0-10s) → Close-Up (10-20s) → Hero Medium (20-30s)?
- [ ] **Zero Watermarks**: No raw prompt tokens or leaked hex codes on-screen?
- [ ] **Clean Outro Buffer**: Does speech finish at ~28.5s with a 1.0s natural hold?
- [ ] **Timeline Assembly**: Click Clip 2 timeline segment (10-20s) before adding Clip 3 to prevent misplaced insertion?
- [ ] **Same Session**: All 3 clips generated and assembled in ONE session?
